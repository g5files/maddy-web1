Outdoor Supervision

Morning Recess

Starts 7:15
Whistle 7:38

Bouldering Cube/Monkey Bar

  • Open the door at 7:37 AM and close it when all students are in.
  • Stay by the door until all students have gone upstairs.
  • Check that the area is clear and everyone has transitioned safely before leaving.

Four Squares

  • Remind students to place bags in the designated area: Lane 10 and beyond.
  • After the whistle, stand in front of the Grade 2 bags as students go upstairs so they do not cross or step into that area.

Basketball Court

  • On odd days, Grade 5 students may play.
  • On even days, Grade 5 may play only if Grades 3 and 4 are not using the court. Give Grades 3 and 4 priority if they want to play.
  • Stay until all Grade 5 students leave the court and head upstairs. Sweep the field for anyone left behind.
  • Stop and redirect running or behavior that does not meet expectations.

Snack Recess

Starts 8:43
Whistle 8:58

Four Squares/Tech Cube

  • This is the only place Grade 5 students may eat snacks.
  • Remind students to dispose of trash and keep the area clean.

Upper Field

  • No eating is allowed on the field. Direct students who are eating there to the designated eating area.

Lunch Recess

Starts 11:50
Students’ Out 12:05
Whistle 12:40

Inside the cafeteria

  • If the serving line has no monitor or fewer than two TAs and you are already in the cafeteria, help supervise the serving area. Make sure students walk, wait their turn, do not cut in line, and follow cafeteria expectations.
  • Once most students are seated and eating, return to your assigned duty spot. Calmly redirect running, excessive noise, or other behavior that does not meet expectations. If a student runs, have them go back and walk.

Food stays inside. Students must finish eating in the cafeteria before leaving. Ask students eating in a hallway to stop and finish in the designated eating area.

On the playground

Stand somewhere visible and accessible, with a clear view of students—not behind pillars or walls. Be ready to step in and redirect students when needed.

Late Start Days

Starts 8:55
Whistle 9:25
  • Usually, only one Grade 5 TA is assigned to recess duty at the Basketball Court.
  • After the whistle, stay until students begin leaving the court and heading upstairs.
  • Do not leave too early. Stand in front of the door to help manage the larger flow of students on the stairs; use the same door as during lunch recess.
  • If two or three Grade 5 TAs are assigned, use the usual locations: Basketball Court, Four Squares, and Bouldering Cube / Monkey Bars. Always check the Child Care Duty for Late Start Days Schedule.

After-School Duty

2:50–3:00 PM: One member of the cafeteria duty pair supervises students at the snack bar entrance while they wait. Lower School students, including those with parents, wait until 3:00 PM to enter the snack bar.

In the afternoon, parents are asked to use the Legacy Commons area. The red Lower School tables and Middle School square tables remain supervised student spaces. Direct parents and guardians away from the red tables to the Upper School area.

Shared-space guidelines

Pick-up first: A Lower School student joining a parent or guardian must first be picked up outside the lobby and accompanied to the cafeteria.

Parent supervision: Parents supervise their own children; staff supervising Upper School students are not responsible for younger children.

Respect the space: Keep voices calm and children walking while Upper School students work.

Students accompanied by parents or guardians do not need to tap in with an ID card.

Coordinate with your duty partner to decide who will collect the laptop from the Guard House.

Health Office Reporting

Call extension #220.

Be ready to report the student’s full name, grade, whether they are conscious or unconscious, and the part of the body injured.
